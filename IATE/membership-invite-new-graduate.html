IATE Membership

IATE membership represents a vital step in your journey as a professional psychotherapist, establishing a robust protective framework for both you and your future clients. As you transition from graduate to practitioner, this membership provides essential support through our Ethics Committee, access to expert resources, and guidance in handling any professional challenges that may arise.

For those working towards UKCP registration, maintaining continuous IATE membership is not just beneficial—it's a mandatory requirement that demonstrates your commitment to professional standards and ongoing development.

Your membership gives you access to valuable benefits including inclusion on IATE's approved therapist lists, discounted professional development opportunities through CCMH, and continuous connection to your training organisation, ensuring you have the support needed as you build your therapeutic practice.
